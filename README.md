SolarShare Protocol  
Decentralized Solar Energy Sharing and Reward System on the Stacks Blockchain  


Overview  
SolarShare Protocol is a decentralized Clarity smart contract designed to enable peer-to-peer solar energy sharing, tokenization, and reward distribution on the Stacks blockchain.  

The protocol empowers individuals, communities, and organizations to tokenize their generated solar power, trade excess energy, and earn carbon-neutral rewards for promoting renewable energy sustainability.


Core Functionality  

| Feature | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Energy Tokenization | Converts generated solar power into blockchain-based energy tokens (SST). |
| Peer-to-Peer Exchange | Allows producers to sell or share excess energy directly with consumers. |
| Reward Distribution | Automatically rewards verified producers for their contribution to clean energy. |
| Transparency & Auditability | Provides immutable records of solar transactions for environmental reporting. |
| Governance Ready | Designed for future DAO integration and community-based decision making. |
